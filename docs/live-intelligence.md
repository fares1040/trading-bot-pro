# Live Intelligence

The live track is intentionally layered and provider-neutral until an approved live flow/options provider is selected.

Pipeline:

Provider observations → normalization → freshness → market pulse → acceleration → opportunity → flow pressure → smart-money evidence → options-flow evidence.

## Safety contract

- Missing values remain null/unknown.
- Stale observations are excluded from derived live decisions.
- Price, volume, notional, options premium, volume, or open interest do not independently prove institutional or whale activity.
- `CONFIRMED` smart-money attribution requires explicit provider attribution evidence.
- No C7–C10 score mutation is performed by the live track.
- No paid or streaming provider is introduced without explicit approval.
